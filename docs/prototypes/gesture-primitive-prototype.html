One Gesture session, or separate hooks that reuse helpers?

Both designs share code. The real question is who owns the lifecycle order: what has to happen on release, on cancel and on lost capture, the "run cleanup only once" guard, and detaching the scroll listener. Under separate hooks + helpers (a′), each hook wires that order itself. Under one session + kinds (b), it is written once, and a kind supplies only two pure functions. Push the same pointer events through both, and flip the "forgot one obligation" switch to see what that costs.
